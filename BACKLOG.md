# Product Backlog & Sprints
# United Pakistan — Dawati Management System (DMS)

## Sprint Overview

- **Sprint 0: Foundation** — Repository, Monorepo Setup, TypeScript, Express, Mongoose, Vite React, Tailwind & shadcn/ui foundation.
- **Sprint 1: Authentication & Workspaces** — User auth (JWT/cookies), multi-tenant workspace isolation, Spectator access-request links, approvals & revocations.
- **Sprint 2: Organizational Core** — Units & Unit officeholders, Centralized People Directory, Multiple Responsibilities, Rabtakar/Daeen/Madu/MDF relationship mapping.
- **Sprint 3: Lifecycle & Activity Engine** — Madu to MDF promotion, MDF to Rabtakar promotion, Unit transfer with historical lock, Dynamic Activity Engine with configurable thresholds.
- **Sprint 4: Individual Meetings** — Mobile-first rapid meeting recording, duplicate prevention, multi-Daeen attribution, meeting history, unit-at-time attribution.
- **Sprint 5: DMM Planning** — Monthly planning sheets, target entries, sector summaries, MSC/MDF expected promotion counts, actual vs planned variance calculations.
- **Sprint 6: RMM Planning** — Weekly unit planning, next-week targets, target vs actual comparisons, progress tracking.
- **Sprint 7: Dialogue Forums & Group Meetings** — DF Mentors directory, DF and GM session recording, participant attendance lists, independent DF & GM analytics.
- **Sprint 8: Reporting & Analytics** — Sector executive dashboard, Weekly Dawati Report, Monthly report, custom date ranges, WhatsApp text clipboard export, PDF/Excel/Word generation.
- **Sprint 9: Production Readiness & Handover** — Security testing, workspace isolation audit, mobile QA, CI/CD with GitHub Actions, free-tier deployment configurations.

## Unresolved Requirements Backlog (Configurable / Extensible)
1. **MSC Organizational Definition**: Retained as a numeric reporting field in DMM; awaiting formal promotion rules.
2. **Default Activity Thresholds**: User-configurable via Workspace Settings.
3. **Spectator Download Permissions**: Default to view-only; permission toggle in Workspace Settings.
4. **Meeting Expected Day in RMM**: Optional text field rather than rigid calendar slot.
